# Research Radar: AI & Bio

Short description (80 characters maximum):
Track biomedical AI conferences, deadlines, workshops and courses worldwide.

Full description:
Research Radar helps biomedical researchers plan their next submission or training opportunity. Browse conferences, workshops and advanced courses across bioinformatics, structural biology, genomics, medical imaging, machine learning for health and AI for biology.

• See event locations, dates and abstract, paper or application deadlines.
• Filter by country, topic, event type, relevance and editorial field tier.
• Explore venues on an interactive world map without sharing your location.
• Save a personal shortlist on your device.
• Export filtered events to CSV or upcoming deadlines as an ICS calendar file.
• Read organizer sources and clearly marked date uncertainties.
• Browse a bundled catalogue offline and retrieve updated information online.

The catalogue is reviewed by a scheduled research task and the app checks for changes while in use. It does not send push notifications. Tiers are editorial assessments within each research field, not official rankings. Always confirm deadlines directly with the organizer before submitting or booking travel.

No account required. No ads or in-app purchases. Research Radar is an independent tracker and is not affiliated with the listed conferences or institutions.

Suggested category: Education
Audience: Adults, academic researchers and graduate students (18+)
Pricing: Free
Release notes: First Android release with offline catalogue, live data refresh, interactive world map, field tiers, country filters, shortlist and CSV/calendar export.
