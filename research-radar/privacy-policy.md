# Research Radar privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026. Research Radar is maintained by Akash Bahai.

Research Radar displays public conference and training information. No account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK is included. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or calendar access.

## Information on your device
Your shortlist and cached catalogue stay in local app storage and are not sent to the developer. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove them. CSV/calendar exports are created only when you choose to export, using Android’s file picker to select the destination.

## Network requests and external links
The app retrieves the public catalogue from GitHub at raw.githubusercontent.com. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as IP address and request metadata, under [GitHub’s privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The developer does not receive app usage analytics. Organizer links open in the system browser and those websites have their own policies. Map geometry is bundled; the app does not contact a map provider or use your location.

## Updates
A scheduled research task reviews the public catalogue. The app checks it when opened and periodically while in use. No push notification service is included. Confirm deadline details with the organizer.

## Contact
For privacy questions use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/akashbahai/test/issues). Issues are public: do not post personal or sensitive information there.
