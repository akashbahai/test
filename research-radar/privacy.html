Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026. Research Radar is maintained by Akash Bahai.

Research Radar displays public conference and training information. No account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK is included. The app does not request your location, contacts, camera or calendar access.

Information on your device

Your shortlist and cached event catalogue are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove them. CSV and calendar exports are created only when you choose to export and are saved to the location you select using Android’s file picker.

Network requests and external links

The app retrieves the public catalogue from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request metadata, under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive app usage analytics. Opening organizer links launches your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies. Map geometry is bundled and does not contact a mapping provider or use your location.

Data updates

The public catalogue is reviewed by a scheduled research task and fetched when the app opens or refreshes while in use. This is not a background notification service. Dates may change; confirm with the organizer before submitting or booking travel.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post personal or sensitive information there.
